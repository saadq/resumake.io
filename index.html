Now in beta

The new Resumake is ready to try at beta.resumake.io. Fill in your details, watch your resume update as you type, and download a clean PDF. No sign-up, no watermark, no catch.

  • 13 templatesIncluding the classic designs from the original Resumake, with a full-size preview before you pick one.
  • Drag to reorderMove whole sections or single entries, and the page follows instantly.
  • Spacing you controlTighten or loosen the whole page, or just one section.
  • More sectionsVolunteer work, certifications, publications, or a section of your own.
  • Back up anytimeExport to the standard JSON Resume format and pick up on any computer.
  • Private by designYour resume is saved in your own browser. The server keeps nothing.

It's a beta, so you may run into rough edges. If something looks off, please tell me with the form at the bottom of this page. The Resumake you know will always stay free.

A note from Saad

Thank you

After years of helping people build resumes, Resumake went quiet for a while, and now it's back. As the next chapter begins, I want to take a moment to thank every single person who used this little tool over the years.

I built Resumake out of a pure love of coding and open source. As a student, I was frustrated by how much time and effort it took to put together a decent resume. I figured if I needed something simpler, others probably did too. So I built it: a free, open-source resume generator that had no ads, required no user accounts, and collected no personal data. It was the kind of software I wished more of the world had. And being open source meant I got to collaborate with other developers who cared about the same things.

I never made money from Resumake. But what I got in return was something far more meaningful. Over the years, I watched this small project grow in ways I never could have imagined. It was featured in a Mashable article and shared across the world. Hundreds of thousands of people used it to build their resumes, and many took the time to send thank-you emails, share kind words, and some even donated. Every one of those messages meant the world to me.

It was beautiful to see how many lives it helped. People landing their first jobs, students applying to internships, career changers putting themselves out there. That was always the point. Not profit, not growth metrics, just making something useful and putting it out there for free.

This was never goodbye. It's a new beginning, and I hope you'll give the beta a try. Please feel free to leave a message at the bottom, I'd really like to hear from you.

With love,
Saad Q.
